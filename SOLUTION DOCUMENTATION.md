&#x20;       **Solution**



&#x20;           **\*The Hospital Bed Availability Board is a web application that helps hospitals and the public know the real-time availability of hospital beds.**



**Key Features:**

**-------------**

&#x20; **Bed Status Management (CRUD):**



**Add new bed details.**



**Update bed availability.**



**Delete bed records.**



**View all bed information.**





&#x20; **ICU and Ward Filters:**

**------------------------**

**Filter available beds by:**



**ICU**



**General Ward**



**Emergency Ward**







&#x20; **Public Map:**

**--------------**

**Show hospital locations on a map.**



**Help users find nearby hospitals.**





&#x20; **Update API:**

**-------------**

**Allow hospitals to update bed information automatically.**



**Keep bed availability up to date.**





&#x20; **Audit Log:**

**-------------**

**Record every update made to the system.**



**Show who updated the data and when.**





&#x20; **Role-Based Access:**

**---------------------**

**Admin: Can manage all bed details.**



**Hospital Staff: Can update bed availability.**



**Public User: Can only view bed availability.**







**Benefits:**

**---------**

&#x20;    **\*Helps patients find available beds quickly.**



&#x20;    **\*Reduces delays during emergencies.**



&#x20;    **\*Improves hospital resource management.**



&#x20;    **\*Provides accurate and real-time bed information.**



&#x20;    **\*Makes the system simple and easy to use.**
